# Data availability statement

Publicly available datasets were analyzed in this study. The AD and DLB summary statistics can be found in the GWAS Catalog under GCST90027158 (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST90027158) and GCST90001390 (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST90001390). The MDD (without 23andMe), PTSD and SCZ summary statistics were obtained from the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium (https://pgc.unc.edu/for-researchers/download-results/; doi: 10.6084/m9.figshare.27061255, 10.6084/m9.figshare.26349322 and 10.6084/m9.figshare.19426775). The UK Biobank-only insomnia file was obtained from the Complex Trait Genetics lab (https://cncr.nl/research/summary_statistics/; doi: 10.5281/zenodo.24366723). FinnGen release 13 results for the endpoint G6_ALZHEIMER are available at https://www.finngen.fi/en/access_results. The LAVA UK Biobank and 1000 Genomes LD reference files are distributed with the LAVA software (https://github.com/josefin-werme/LAVA), and the clumping reference by the MRC Integrative Epidemiology Unit (http://fileserve.mrcieu.ac.uk/ld/1kg.v3.tgz). The analysis code and the summary results generated in this study are available at [REPOSITORY_DOI].

# Ethics statement

Ethical approval was not required for this study, because it used only publicly available GWAS summary statistics and no individual-level data. Ethical approval and informed consent for the original studies were obtained by the respective studies, as reported in their publications.
