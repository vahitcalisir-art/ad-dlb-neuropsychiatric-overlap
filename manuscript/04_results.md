# Results

## Genome-wide genetic correlation

Every trait except DLB had a SNP heritability estimate distinguishable from zero, and the DLB heritability Z score of {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}} fell below the threshold under which the analysis plan flags rg as low power (Figure 2). Heritability Z scores were {{res:ldsc_h2.main.ad.h2_z}} for AD and ranged from {{res:ldsc_h2.main.insomnia.h2_z}} to {{res:ldsc_h2.main.mdd.h2_z}} across the psychiatric traits.

There was no evidence of genetic correlation between any of the four psychiatric traits and AD (Figure 2). Point estimates were {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.mdd.rg}} for MDD, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.ptsd.rg}} for PTSD, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.insomnia.rg}} for insomnia and {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.scz.rg}} for SCZ, with FDR-corrected P values of {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.mdd.p_fdr}}, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.ptsd.p_fdr}}, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.insomnia.p_fdr}} and {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.scz.p_fdr}}. Depending on the trait, the upper confidence limits excluded positive correlations with AD larger than {{res:power_ldsc.ad.ptsd.excluded_above}} to {{res:power_ldsc.ad.scz.excluded_above}}.

DLB was positively correlated with MDD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.rg}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.p_fdr}}) and with PTSD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.rg}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.p_fdr}}). These estimates rest on a DLB heritability flagged as low power (Z {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}}), and their lower confidence limits lay close to zero ({{res:power_ldsc.dlb.mdd.ci_lower}} and {{res:power_ldsc.dlb.ptsd.ci_lower}}). Correlations of DLB with insomnia ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.insomnia.rg}}) and SCZ ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.scz.rg}}) were not distinguishable from zero and had wide intervals. In the secondary family, AD and DLB were correlated ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.dlb.rg}}).

In the pre-specified sensitivity analyses the AD estimates changed little (Supplementary Table S4). Using MDD including UK Biobank gave an rg with AD of {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_mdd_ukbb.ad.mdd_ukbb.rg}}, the PTSD case/control file gave {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_ptsd_cc.ad.ptsd_cc.rg}}, and after removal of the APOE region from AD the estimates ranged from {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_noapoe.ad_noapoe.ptsd.rg}} for PTSD to {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_noapoe.ad_noapoe.scz.rg}} for SCZ. The alternative MDD and PTSD files gave similar DLB estimates ({{res:ldsc_rg.sens_mdd_ukbb.dlb.mdd_ukbb.rg}} and {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_ptsd_cc.dlb.ptsd_cc.rg}}).

With FinnGen AD, the correlation with PTSD was positive ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.rg}}; uncorrected P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.p_uncorrected}}) but did not survive correction across the four FinnGen tests (BH-adjusted P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.p_bh_within4}}). Correlations of FinnGen AD with MDD ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.mdd.rg}}), insomnia ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.insomnia.rg}}) and SCZ ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.scz.rg}}) were close to those obtained with the primary AD GWAS.

## Reference panel sensitivity analysis

The 1000 Genomes analysis was a pre-specified sensitivity analysis, and its results were not used to declare findings (Supplementary Tables S6 and S7). Results differed substantially between reference panels (Supplementary Table S6). With that reference, {{res:lava_g1000.summary.bivar_tests_primary}} primary bivariate tests were possible and {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} locus–pairs passed FDR correction (Supplementary Table S6). Summed local heritability over loci analysed with both references was higher with 1000 Genomes for every trait, by a factor of {{res:lava_reference_diagnostic.supplementary.range}} (Supplementary Table S7). Of the {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} FDR-significant pairs, {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_tested_ukb}} was testable with UK Biobank and nominal there, namely the AD × SCZ locus on chromosome 16 ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.2128.ad_scz.locus}}; UK Biobank rho {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.locus.2128.ad_scz.rho_ukb}}, P {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.locus.2128.ad_scz.p_ukb}}). The remaining {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_not_tested_ukb}} could not be tested with UK Biobank because at least one trait did not pass the univariate filter. The chromosome 17 loci were nominal with UK Biobank and kept their sign with 1000 Genomes, but they did not pass FDR correction with either reference (Supplementary Table S6).

As a check, the strand-mismatch exclusion used for the 1000 Genomes inputs was also applied to the main analysis (Supplementary Table S8). Across the {{res:lava_strand_check.summary.n_pairs}} locus–pairs that passed FDR correction in the secondary family of the main analysis, {{res:text.strand_check_changed}}.

## Mendelian randomization

Table 2 gives instrument numbers, strength and diagnostics for the sixteen primary directions, and Figure 4 shows the estimates. PTSD → AD was the only primary direction that passed FDR correction (OR {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.or_ivw}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}). It was classified as sensitive to pleiotropy on the basis of the SIMEX MR-Egger intercept (P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.simex_intercept_p}}), which was used because I²GX was {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.isq_gx}}, and its overlap-corrected (MRlap) estimate was not nominally significant (standardized beta {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.beta_std_mrlap_corrected}}, P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_mrlap_corrected}}). With FinnGen as the outcome source, the IVW estimate was not nominally significant (OR {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}; P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.p_ivw}}).

{{table:table2_mr_diagnostics.csv|Instrument and sensitivity diagnostics of the sixteen primary MR directions; the IVW odds ratios are shown in Figure 4. MR-Egger intercept P is from the SIMEX-corrected model when I²GX < 0.9. MRlap estimates are standardized betas, not odds ratios. Class follows the pre-specified interpretation rule.}}

Genetic liability to SCZ was nominally associated with AD (OR {{res:mr.scz__ad.or_ivw}}; P {{res:mr.scz__ad.p_ivw}}), but the association did not pass FDR correction (FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.scz__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}) and was classified as no evidence. No association with AD was seen for liability to MDD (OR {{res:mr.mdd__ad.or_ivw}}) or insomnia (OR {{res:mr.insomnia__ad.or_ivw}}). No psychiatric trait was associated with DLB, although the minimum detectable ORs for these directions were large, from {{res:mr.scz__dlb.min_detectable_or}} for SCZ to {{res:mr.insomnia__dlb.min_detectable_or}} for insomnia (Table 2).

In the reverse direction, AD liability showed no association with MDD ({{res:mr.ad__mdd.or_ivw}}), PTSD ({{res:mr.ad__ptsd.or_ivw}}), insomnia ({{res:mr.ad__insomnia.or_ivw}}) or SCZ ({{res:mr.ad__scz.or_ivw}}). For AD → insomnia, the MRlap-corrected estimate was nominally significant (standardized beta {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.beta_std_mrlap_corrected}}; P {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.p_mrlap_corrected}}), whereas the IVW estimate was not (P {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.p_ivw}}). DLB liability, instrumented by few variants (Table 2), showed no association with any psychiatric trait.
