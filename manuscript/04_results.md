# Results

## Genome-wide genetic correlation

All six traits had SNP heritability estimates distinguishable from zero except DLB, whose heritability Z score was {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}}, below the threshold under which the analysis plan flags rg as low power (Figure 2). The heritability Z score was {{res:ldsc_h2.main.ad.h2_z}} for AD and ranged from {{res:ldsc_h2.main.insomnia.h2_z}} to {{res:ldsc_h2.main.mdd.h2_z}} for the psychiatric traits.

There was no evidence of genetic correlation between any of the four psychiatric traits and AD (Figure 2). The estimates were {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.mdd.rg}} for MDD, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.ptsd.rg}} for PTSD, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.insomnia.rg}} for insomnia and {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.scz.rg}} for SCZ, with FDR-corrected P values of {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.mdd.p_fdr}}, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.ptsd.p_fdr}}, {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.insomnia.p_fdr}} and {{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.scz.p_fdr}}. The upper confidence limits excluded positive correlations with AD larger than {{res:power_ldsc.ad.ptsd.excluded_above}} to {{res:power_ldsc.ad.scz.excluded_above}}, depending on the trait.

DLB was positively correlated with MDD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.rg}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.p_fdr}}) and with PTSD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.rg}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.p_fdr}}). Both estimates rest on a DLB heritability flagged as low power (Z {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}}), and their lower confidence limits lay close to zero ({{res:power_ldsc.dlb.mdd.ci_lower}} and {{res:power_ldsc.dlb.ptsd.ci_lower}}). DLB was not correlated with insomnia ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.insomnia.rg}}) or SCZ ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.scz.rg}}), with wide intervals in both cases. As a secondary pair, AD and DLB were correlated ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.ad.dlb.rg}}).

The AD estimates changed little in the pre-specified sensitivity analyses (Supplementary Table S4). With MDD including UK Biobank, rg with AD was {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_mdd_ukbb.ad.mdd_ukbb.rg}}; with the PTSD case/control file it was {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_ptsd_cc.ad.ptsd_cc.rg}}. After removal of the APOE region from AD, rg ranged from {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_noapoe.ad_noapoe.ptsd.rg}} for PTSD to {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_noapoe.ad_noapoe.scz.rg}} for SCZ. The DLB × MDD and DLB × PTSD estimates were similar with the alternative MDD and PTSD files ({{res:ldsc_rg.sens_mdd_ukbb.dlb.mdd_ukbb.rg}} and {{res:ldsc_rg.sens_ptsd_cc.dlb.ptsd_cc.rg}}).

With FinnGen AD, the correlation with PTSD was positive ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.rg}}; uncorrected P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.p_uncorrected}}), but it did not survive correction across the four FinnGen tests (BH-adjusted P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.ptsd.p_bh_within4}}). The FinnGen correlations with MDD ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.mdd.rg}}), insomnia ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.insomnia.rg}}) and SCZ ({{res:sensitivity_finngen.ldsc.finngen.scz.rg}}) were close to those obtained with the primary AD GWAS.

## Local genetic correlation

Of the {{res:lava_ukb.summary.loci_analysed}} loci analysed with the UK Biobank reference, few carried local heritability for both members of a primary pair, so {{res:lava_ukb.summary.bivar_tests_primary}} bivariate tests were possible across the eight primary pairs (Figure 3). None reached FDR significance ({{res:lava_ukb.summary.sig_fdr_primary}} locus–pairs). The number of testable loci was {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.mdd.loci_testable}} for AD × MDD, {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.ptsd.loci_testable}} for AD × PTSD, {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.insomnia.loci_testable}} for AD × insomnia and {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.scz.loci_testable}} for AD × SCZ. Insomnia passed the univariate filter at only {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.insomnia.loci_univ_psychiatric}} loci, and DLB at {{res:power_lava_ukb.dlb.mdd.loci_univ_dementia}}, so the DLB pairs had between {{res:power_lava_ukb.dlb.ptsd.loci_testable}} and {{res:power_lava_ukb.dlb.mdd.loci_testable}} testable loci.

Nominal local correlations were observed at {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.mdd.loci_nominal_p05}} of {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.mdd.loci_testable}} testable loci for AD × MDD (expected under the null {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.mdd.expected}}), {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.scz.loci_nominal_p05}} of {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.scz.loci_testable}} for AD × SCZ (expected {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.scz.expected}}) and {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.ptsd.loci_nominal_p05}} of {{res:power_lava_ukb.ad.ptsd.loci_testable}} for AD × PTSD (expected {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.ptsd.expected}}). These loci are exploratory and are listed in Supplementary Table S5. For AD × MDD, {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.mdd.nominal_positive_rho}} nominal correlations were positive and {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.mdd.nominal_negative_rho}} negative. For AD × SCZ, {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.scz.nominal_positive_rho}} of {{res:lava_exploratory.ad.scz.nominal_p05}} nominal correlations were positive.

A locus on chromosome 17 containing MAPT, KANSL1 and CRHR1 ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.2207.ad_mdd.locus}}) was nominal for AD × MDD, AD × PTSD and AD × SCZ. Local rho was {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_mdd.rho_ukb}} for AD × MDD, {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_ptsd.rho_ukb}} for AD × PTSD and {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_scz.rho_ukb}} for AD × SCZ. With the 1000 Genomes reference the corresponding estimates were {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_mdd.rho_g1000}}, {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_ptsd.rho_g1000}} and {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2207.ad_scz.rho_g1000}}, each with the same sign and within the UK Biobank confidence interval. The adjacent locus ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.2206.ad_mdd.locus}}) behaved the same way for AD × MDD ({{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2206.ad_mdd.rho_ukb}} and {{res:lava_reference_check.locus.2206.ad_mdd.rho_g1000}}). A locus on chromosome 11 containing SPI1 and CELF1 ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.1658.ad_mdd.locus}}), a known AD risk region [@bellenguez2022new#c10], was nominal for AD × MDD ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.1658.ad_mdd.rho}}) but was not testable with the 1000 Genomes reference, because a trait did not pass the univariate filter there.

## Reference panel sensitivity analysis

The 1000 Genomes analysis was a pre-specified sensitivity analysis, and its results were not used to declare findings (Supplementary Tables S6 and S7). Results differed substantially between reference panels (Supplementary Table S6). With the 1000 Genomes reference, {{res:lava_g1000.summary.bivar_tests_primary}} primary bivariate tests were possible and {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} locus–pairs passed FDR correction (Supplementary Table S6). Summed local heritability over loci analysed with both references was higher with 1000 Genomes for every trait, by a factor of {{res:lava_reference_diagnostic.supplementary.range}} (Supplementary Table S7). Of the {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} FDR-significant pairs, {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_tested_ukb}} was testable with UK Biobank and was nominal there: the AD × SCZ locus on chromosome 16 ({{res:lava_exploratory.locus.2128.ad_scz.locus}}; UK Biobank rho {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.locus.2128.ad_scz.rho_ukb}}, P {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.locus.2128.ad_scz.p_ukb}}). The remaining {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_not_tested_ukb}} were not testable with UK Biobank because at least one trait did not pass the univariate filter. The chromosome 17 loci were nominal with UK Biobank and had the same sign with 1000 Genomes, but they did not pass FDR correction with either reference (Supplementary Table S6).

The strand-mismatch exclusion applied to the 1000 Genomes inputs was checked against the main analysis (Supplementary Table S8). Across the {{res:lava_strand_check.summary.n_pairs}} locus–pairs that passed FDR correction in the secondary family of the main analysis, the number whose sign or significance changed after the exclusion was {{res:lava_strand_check.summary.n_changed}}.

## Colocalization

Colocalization was not performed, because its pre-specified condition was not met: no primary locus–pair passed FDR correction in the main LAVA analysis ({{res:coloc.summary.lava_primary_sig_fdr}} of {{res:coloc.summary.lava_primary_tests}} locus–pairs).

## Mendelian randomization

Instrument numbers, strength and diagnostics for the sixteen primary directions are given in Table 2, and the estimates are shown in Figure 4. The only primary direction that passed FDR correction (PTSD → AD, OR {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.or_ivw}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}) was classified as sensitive to pleiotropy, and the overlap-corrected (MRlap) estimate was not nominally significant (standardized beta {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.beta_std_mrlap_corrected}}, P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_mrlap_corrected}}). With FinnGen as the outcome source, the IVW estimate was not nominally significant (OR {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}; P {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.p_ivw}}). The pleiotropy classification followed from the SIMEX MR-Egger intercept (P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.simex_intercept_p}}), used because I²GX was {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.isq_gx}}.

MR-Egger intercept P is from the SIMEX-corrected model when I²GX < 0.9. MRlap estimates are standardized betas, not odds ratios. Class follows the pre-specified interpretation rule.}}

Genetic liability to SCZ was nominally associated with AD (OR {{res:mr.scz__ad.or_ivw}}; P {{res:mr.scz__ad.p_ivw}}), but the association did not pass FDR correction (FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.scz__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}) and was classified as no evidence. Genetic liability to MDD (OR {{res:mr.mdd__ad.or_ivw}}) and insomnia (OR {{res:mr.insomnia__ad.or_ivw}}) showed no association with AD. No psychiatric trait was associated with DLB, but the minimum detectable ORs for these directions were large, from {{res:mr.scz__dlb.min_detectable_or}} for SCZ to {{res:mr.insomnia__dlb.min_detectable_or}} for insomnia (Table 2).

In the reverse direction, genetic liability to AD showed no association with MDD ({{res:mr.ad__mdd.or_ivw}}), PTSD ({{res:mr.ad__ptsd.or_ivw}}), insomnia ({{res:mr.ad__insomnia.or_ivw}}) or SCZ ({{res:mr.ad__scz.or_ivw}}). For AD → insomnia, the MRlap-corrected estimate was nominally significant (standardized beta {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.beta_std_mrlap_corrected}}; P {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.p_mrlap_corrected}}) while the IVW estimate was not (P {{res:mr.ad__insomnia.p_ivw}}). DLB liability, instrumented by few variants (Table 2), showed no association with any psychiatric trait.

Steiger filtering removed {{res:mr_steiger_prevalence.summary.mdd_dlb_removed_k1}} of {{res:mr_steiger_prevalence.summary.mdd_dlb_harmonised}} harmonized instruments for MDD → DLB (Table 2). With every prevalence halved or doubled, the number removed was {{res:mr_steiger_prevalence.summary.mdd_dlb_removed_half}} and {{res:mr_steiger_prevalence.summary.mdd_dlb_removed_double}}, and across the eight DLB directions the number of removed instruments changed by at most {{res:mr_steiger_prevalence.summary.max_change_in_removed}}, so the IVW estimates were unchanged (Supplementary Table S9).

Exclusion of APOE-region instruments did not change the estimates for psychiatric exposures, whose instruments did not include the APOE region; PTSD → AD remained {{res:mr.ptsd__ad__noapoe.or_ivw}}. For AD as exposure, the APOE-excluded estimates were {{res:mr.ad__mdd__noapoe.or_ivw}} for MDD, {{res:mr.ad__ptsd__noapoe.or_ivw}} for PTSD, {{res:mr.ad__insomnia__noapoe.or_ivw}} for insomnia and {{res:mr.ad__scz__noapoe.or_ivw}} for SCZ (Supplementary Table S10). With MDD including UK Biobank, MDD → AD was {{res:mr.mdd_ukbb__ad.or_ivw}} and AD → MDD {{res:mr.ad__mdd_ukbb.or_ivw}}.

In the FinnGen consistency analysis, no psychiatric trait was associated with FinnGen AD: MDD {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.mdd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}, PTSD {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}, insomnia {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.insomnia__ad_finngen.or_ivw}} and SCZ {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.scz__ad_finngen.or_ivw}} (Figure 4C). In the reverse direction the FinnGen estimates were close to the null, from {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ad_finngen__mdd.or_ivw}} for MDD to {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ad_finngen__scz.or_ivw}} for SCZ.
