Genome-wide, local and bidirectional MR analyses provided no robust evidence of genetic overlap or causal effects between MDD, PTSD, insomnia or SCZ and AD (Figures 2, 3 and Table 2). The only MR signal, PTSD → AD, was sensitive to pleiotropy and to sample overlap and was not significant with FinnGen AD as the outcome (Figure 4C). The lack of evidence for shared genetic liability is consistent with the view that depression in the years before dementia can be a symptom of the evolving disease [@livingston2024dementia#c3]. The DLB correlations with MDD and PTSD passed FDR correction but rest on a DLB heritability estimate with low power (Figure 2), and their interpretation will require a substantially larger DLB GWAS [@watkins2026understanding#c1].
