# Abstract

Neuropsychiatric symptoms are common in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), and psychiatric disorders earlier in life are associated with later dementia, although observational data cannot rule out reverse causation <!--from:intro_nps--> <!--from:intro_psych--> <!--from:intro_revcaus-->. Genome-wide genetic correlations of AD with psychiatric traits have been close to zero, yet local signals of opposite sign can cancel in such estimates <!--from:intro_null--> <!--from:intro_local-->. Genetic data on DLB remain limited <!--from:intro_dlb-->. Summary statistics from genome-wide association studies (GWAS) of major depressive disorder (MDD), post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), insomnia and schizophrenia (SCZ) were analyzed against AD and DLB with LD score regression, local genetic correlation and bidirectional two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR) <!--from:intro_aim-->. Sensitivity analyses excluded the APOE region and used a register-based AD GWAS without proxy cases (FinnGen) <!--from:intro_aim-->. No psychiatric trait showed evidence of genome-wide genetic correlation with AD, and the upper confidence limits excluded correlations above {{res:power_ldsc.ad.ptsd.excluded_above}} to {{res:power_ldsc.ad.scz.excluded_above}}. Of the {{res:lava_ukb.summary.bivar_tests_primary}} testable local correlations, none passed false discovery rate (FDR) correction. Among the sixteen MR directions, PTSD → AD alone passed FDR correction (odds ratio {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.or_ivw}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}), but it was classified as sensitive to pleiotropy and lost nominal significance after correction for sample overlap (P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_mrlap_corrected}}). With FinnGen AD as the outcome, the same direction was not significant (odds ratio {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}). For DLB, the genome-wide correlations with MDD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.rg}}) and PTSD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.rg}}) passed FDR correction (FDR-corrected P {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.p_fdr}}), but they rest on a DLB heritability estimate with low power (Z {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}}). With a 1000 Genomes LD reference, {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} local locus–pairs passed FDR correction, and {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_not_tested_ukb}} of them could not be tested with the UK Biobank reference of the main analysis. The results provide no robust evidence of genetic overlap or causal effects between these psychiatric traits and AD, and the borderline DLB correlations require confirmation in a larger DLB GWAS <!--from:disc_dlb_confirm-->.
