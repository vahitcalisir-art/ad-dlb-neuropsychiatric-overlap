# Abstract

Neuropsychiatric symptoms are common in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), and psychiatric disorders earlier in life are associated with later dementia, but reverse causation limits what observational studies can show <!--from:intro_nps--> <!--from:intro_psych--> <!--from:intro_revcaus-->. Genome-wide genetic correlations between AD and psychiatric traits have been close to zero, opposing local signals can cancel in such estimates, and genetic data on DLB are limited <!--from:intro_null--> <!--from:intro_local--> <!--from:intro_dlb-->. Genome-wide association study (GWAS) summary statistics for major depressive disorder (MDD), post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), insomnia and schizophrenia (SCZ) were analyzed against AD and DLB with LD score regression, local genetic correlation and bidirectional two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR) <!--from:intro_aim-->. Sensitivity analyses excluded the APOE region and used a clinically diagnosed AD GWAS without proxy cases <!--from:intro_aim-->. There was no evidence of genome-wide genetic correlation between the psychiatric traits and AD, and the upper confidence limits excluded correlations above {{res:power_ldsc.ad.ptsd.excluded_above}} to {{res:power_ldsc.ad.scz.excluded_above}}. None of the {{res:lava_ukb.summary.bivar_tests_primary}} testable local correlations passed false discovery rate (FDR) correction. Of the sixteen MR directions, only PTSD → AD passed FDR correction (odds ratio {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.or_ivw}}; FDR-corrected P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_ivw_fdr}}). That estimate was sensitive to pleiotropy, its overlap-corrected version was not nominally significant (P {{res:mr.ptsd__ad.p_mrlap_corrected}}), and it was not significant with FinnGen AD as outcome (odds ratio {{res:sensitivity_finngen.mr.ptsd__ad_finngen.or_ivw}}). DLB was genetically correlated with MDD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.rg}}) and PTSD ({{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.ptsd.rg}}) at an FDR-corrected P of {{res:ldsc_rg.main.dlb.mdd.p_fdr}}, but the DLB heritability estimate had low power (Z {{res:ldsc_h2.main.dlb.h2_z}}). With a 1000 Genomes LD reference, {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_fdr_pairs_g1000}} local locus–pairs passed FDR correction, of which {{res:lava_g1000_fdr_pairs.summary.n_not_tested_ukb}} were not testable with the UK Biobank reference used in the main analysis. These data give no robust evidence of genetic overlap or causal effects between the four psychiatric traits and AD, and the borderline DLB correlations need confirmation in a larger DLB GWAS <!--from:disc_dlb_confirm-->.
