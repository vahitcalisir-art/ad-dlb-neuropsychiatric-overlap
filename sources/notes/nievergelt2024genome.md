## Bibliographic record
Nievergelt CM, Maihofer AX, Atkinson EG, et al.; 2024; Genome-wide association analyses identify 95 risk loci and provide insights into the neurobiology of posttraumatic stress disorder; Nature Genetics 56(5):792–808; DOI 10.1038/s41588-024-01707-9 (PMID 38637617; PMC11396662; NIH author manuscript); multi-ancestry GWAS meta-analysis (PGC-PTSD Freeze 3) with post-GWAS functional, genetic-architecture, genetic-correlation (LDSC, MiXeR, LAVA) and PRS analyses; access route PMC-XML (published version, author manuscript)

## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: PTSD genetics have low discoverability; multi-ancestry GWAS meta-analysis across 1,222,882 European-ancestry individuals (137,136 cases) and 58,051 admixed African and Native American ancestry individuals (13,624 cases); 95 GWS loci (80 novel); 43 potential causal genes (synaptic/ion channel, developmental/axon guidance, synaptic structure, endocrine/immune, e.g. GRIA1, FOXP2, ESR1, TRAF3).
- Introduction: About 5.6% of trauma-exposed adults worldwide have PTSD in their lifetimes (ref 1). Earlier PGC-PTSD and MVP GWAS (>200,000 people) found at most 16 inconsistent loci and did not examine chromosome X. PTSD is comorbid and genetically correlated with MDD, ADHD, cardiovascular disease and obesity. Freeze 3 combines 88 studies: EA 137,136 cases / 1,085,746 controls; AA 11,560 / 39,474; LAT 2,064 / 4,953.
- Methods: Freeze 3 = 1,307,247 individuals from 88 studies, 95 GWASs, from three sources: (1) clinician-administered or self-report instrument studies (Freeze 2.5 + newer; Ext. Data Fig. 1: 26,080 cases / 192,966 controls); (2) MVP release 3, PCL for DSM-IV (32,372 / 154,317); (3) 10 EHR biobanks — BioVU, Mass General Brigham, BioMe, MayoGC, iPSYCH, FinnGen, HUNT, STR-STAGE, UK Biobank, Estonia Biobank — with broad case definition (≥1 PTSD or other stress-disorder ICD-9/10 code; all others controls): 78,687 cases of 817,181 (9.6%) (Ext. Data Fig. 1 gives 78,684 cases / 738,463 controls). Lifetime vs current definitions not set out in main text (Supplementary Note). Ancestry: SNPweights (EA ≥ 90% European), MVP PCA on 1000G, EHR own protocols. RICOPILI QC; HRC imputation (central) or 1000G/HRC/SISu/composite (external); all lifted to GRCh37/hg19. Per-study GWAS (PLINK with 5 PCs; BOLT-LMM for UKBB; SAIGE/REGENIE for EHR), sex-stratified; X chromosome with sex covariate. METAL sample-size-weighted fixed effects; MAF ≥ 1%, INFO ≥ 0.6; markers in < 80% of total Neff removed. LDSC h2 with KGP3 European LD scores, HapMap3 SNPs, MHC excluded; MiXeR; LAVA local rg PTSD–MDD (MDD GWAS ref 50; KGP3 European LD reference; 2,495 blocks); Pan-UKB rg (EUR n = 420,531); PRS-CS in MVP holdouts. Summary statistics: PGC website, accession ptsd2024.
- Results: EA meta: GC lambda 1.55, LDSC intercept 1.0524 (s.e. 0.0097), attenuation ratio 0.0729 → 92.7% of inflation polygenic. 81 EA loci (5 on X), 67 novel; AA and LAT no GWS loci; multi-ancestry (150,793 cases, 1,130,197 controls) 85 loci; 95 unique loci (Table 1, GRCh37 coordinates). MiXeR: no subset-specific causal variation. h2SNP (LDSC) = 0.053 (s.e. 0.002, P = 6.8 × 10−156) — scale (observed vs liability) and prevalence for h2 not stated. No sex difference (P = 0.13; male–female rg 0.98). ~10,863 influential variants. Moderate-to-high positive rg with other psychiatric disorders (Ext. Data Fig. 7a); MiXeR 79–99% shared with BPD, MDD, SCZ; concordance 87% MDD, 67% BPD, 65% SCZ. rg PTSD–MDD = 0.85 (s.e. 0.008), varying by subset (MVP lowest, biobank highest; Suppl. Table 24). LAVA: 141 regions with significant local h2 for both; 40 significant local rg, all positive (mean 0.57); some PTSD loci uncorrelated with MDD. Pan-UKB: rg with 73% of 1,114 traits (sertraline 0.88; recurrent severe depression 0.87; multi-site chronic pain 0.63; mother's age at death −0.51). No dementia, AD or insomnia rg reported in main text. Brain (cerebellum, cortex, hypothalamus, hippocampus, amygdala) and pituitary enrichment; GABAergic neurons; 17q21.31 (CRHR1, MAPT, KANSL1) in TWAS/SMR. PRS: top vs bottom quintile RR 2.4 (EA); 6.6% liability-scale variance at 15% population and sample prevalence; AA 0.9%. Content and numbers identical to the earlier author-manuscript text; excerpts re-copied from the PMC-XML text.
- Discussion: Largest PTSD GWAS, passes discovery inflection point; cerebellum and interneurons; 17q21.31; no sex difference in heritability despite epidemiological sex differences (ESR1 candidate). Highly shared polygenicity with psychiatric disorders but effect discordance; PTSD–MDD rg can exceed rg between PTSD cohorts with different measures; local rg reveals more heterogeneity than global rg; overlap may reflect general trauma vulnerability; tentative drug-class signals (antipsychotics via DRD2, opioids via CYP2D6).
- Limitations stated by authors: need for more data from under-represented ancestries; PRS transfers poorly to AA/LAT; fine-mapping restricted to EA; MHC LD poorly captured by reference panels; drug-target results with few targets may be false positives; heterogeneous ascertainment/case definitions (PTSD–MDD rg varies by subset).
- Supplementary material: not available (Supplementary Tables/Note referenced only)

## Quality appraisal
Very large sample (EA n = 1,222,882; Neff 641,533); LDSC intercept/attenuation ratio indicate minimal confounding; peer-reviewed (Nature Genetics). Bias risks: heterogeneous phenotype — EHR broad codes include other stress disorders and contribute most EA cases; MVP uses PCL; others clinical or self-report. Biobank cases correlate most with MDD (possible general internalizing signal). UK Biobank and FinnGen among contributing cohorts → possible sample overlap with UKB-based insomnia (Watanabe 2022), the UKB LAVA reference, the FinnGen R13 AD sensitivity outcome and the UKB proxy cases in Bellenguez 2022 (relevant to MR/MRlap). Build GRCh37. h2 scale/prevalence not stated; PRS liability transformation uses 15%, not our 0.07. Several authors report industry ties. Not an MR paper.

## Relevance to this manuscript
- M1 (primary): PTSD GWAS data-source description — sample sizes, EA subset, cohort composition (clinical/self-report studies, MVP PCL, 10 EHR biobanks including UK Biobank and FinnGen), broad ICD case definition, GRCh37, meta-analysis method, Neff.
- I3: genome-wide PTSD–MDD rg 0.85 alongside LAVA local heterogeneity — supports our rationale that genome-wide rg can hide local, disorder-specific signals; MiXeR overlap with SCZ.
- D6: phenotype heterogeneity, EA-only usability, UKB/FinnGen inclusion (sample overlap).
- M3: does NOT support PTSD prevalence 0.07 — cites 5.6% lifetime PTSD among trauma-exposed adults and uses 15% prevalence for PRS liability; a separate primary source is needed.

## Decision
KEEP — the PTSD GWAS data source actually used (PGC-PTSD Freeze 3), peer-reviewed.
