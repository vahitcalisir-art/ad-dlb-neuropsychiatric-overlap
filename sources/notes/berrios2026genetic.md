Berrios W, Golimstok A, Fernández MC; 2026; Genetic susceptibility and environmental factors in dementia with Lewy bodies: Converging pathogenic mechanisms; Frontiers in Neurology 17:1825397; DOI 10.3389/fneur.2026.1825397; mini review (narrative with structured search); access route PMC-XML (PMC13152754)

## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: DLB determinants incompletely defined and often extrapolated from PD; moderate heritability; key loci APOE, GBA, SNCA delineating biologically distinct subgroups; environmental exposures (pesticides, air pollution, heavy metals, endocrine disruptors) mainly from experimental/observational evidence; framework of genetic constraint plus environmental modulation.
- Introduction: DLB second most common neurodegenerative dementia; core features (fluctuations, visual hallucinations, RBD, parkinsonism); 1-year rule vs PDD; high AD co-pathology; systematic review found limited evidence on DLB risk factors (MCI, RBD, HSV infection, adult ADHD); replicated loci APOE, GBA, SNCA; DLB evidence often extrapolated from PD.
- Methods: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Google Scholar Jan 2021 - Jan 2026; narrative, no formal protocol; 26 original studies and 2 systematic reviews selected.
- Results: heritability ~34% from extended family analyses; APOE e4 strongest risk factor for DLB with amyloid co-pathology (DLB-AD subgroup), GBA E326K linked to pure DLB and earlier onset, SNCA dosage/regulatory variation; lipid biology, mitochondrial quality control, neuroinflammation, epigenetics (METTL3, PAK6, LIPA methylation); APOE3-Jacksonville protective; MAP3K15 sex-specific locus. Environmental: paraquat, PM2.5 (cohorts >50 million, hospitalisation risk), lead/copper, phthalates and gut microbiome.
- Discussion: genetic determinants delineate subgroups; 22.3% of 795 cognitively impaired individuals had combined Lewy and AD pathology by CSF/SAA; environmental evidence associative; few studies integrate genes and environment.
- Limitations stated by authors: narrative design; 5-year window; extrapolation from other synucleinopathies; environmental studies lack neuropathological confirmation and genetic stratification; genetic studies lack exposure data; mechanistic evidence from small samples/rare variants.
- Supplementary material: not available (none)
