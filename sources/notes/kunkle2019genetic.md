## Bibliographic record
Kunkle BW, Grenier-Boley B, Sims R, et al.; 2019; Genetic meta-analysis of diagnosed Alzheimer's disease identifies new risk loci and implicates Aβ, tau, immunity and lipid processing; Nature Genetics 51(3):414–430 (Author Correction: Nat Genet 51(9):1423–1424, 2019); DOI 10.1038/s41588-019-0358-2 (PMID 30820047; PMC6463297; NIH author manuscript); three-stage case–control GWAS meta-analysis (IGAP) with gene prioritisation, HLA fine-mapping, pathway and genetic-correlation analyses; access route PMC-XML (published version, author manuscript)

## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: GWAS meta-analysis of clinically diagnosed LOAD (94,437 individuals); confirms 20 previous loci and identifies five new loci (IQCK, ACE, ADAM10, ADAMTS1, WWOX), two of which (ADAM10, ACE) were also found in a GWAS-by-familial-proxy; HLA-DR15 haplotype confirmed; pathways: immunity, lipid metabolism, tau binding, APP metabolism; rare-variant enrichment (P = 1.32 × 10−7); genetic correlations with family history of dementia and education. (Preprint reported 89,769 individuals and four new loci; WWOX, HLA fine-mapping and rg analyses are new in the published version.)
- Introduction: Previous IGAP work found 19 GWS signals plus APOE (onset > 65 years), explaining ~31% of LOAD genetic variance with subthreshold signals. Stage 1 discovery in non-Hispanic Whites: 17 new / 46 total datasets, n = 21,982 cases and 41,944 cognitively normal controls from four IGAP consortia (ADGC, CHARGE, EADI, GERAD/PERADES); 1000 Genomes imputation; 9,456,058 common and 2,024,574 rare variants after QC.
- Methods: Stage 1 samples from ADGC, CHARGE, EADI, GERAD/PERADES (46 case–control studies; demographics and diagnostic details in Supplementary Tables 1–2 and Note); non-European ancestry excluded by PCA; imputation to 1000 Genomes phase 1 integrated release 3 (March 2012) with SHAPEIT/IMPUTE2 or MaCH/Minimac; additive model adjusted for age (age-at-onset for cases, age-at-last-exam for controls), sex and PCs; score test (case–control) and GWAF GEE/GLMM (families); METAL inverse-variance with genomic control; rare variants only in studies with n ≥ 400. LDSC via LD Hub (APOE region removed; MHC and APOE removed for rg; 792 traits incl. 518 UK Biobank traits). Stage 2: I-select chip (11,632 variants), 8,362 cases / 10,483 controls (n = 18,845). Stage 3A: 4,930 cases / 6,736 controls (n = 11,666); Stage 3B: 13,292 / 17,219 (n = 30,511). No sample overlap between stages. Positions build 37 (hg19). GCTA-COJO (28,730 unrelated ADGC reference); eQTL, COLOC/SMR-HEIDI, AlzBase, MAGMA pathways (10,861 sets), Aβ network (335 genes), HLA imputation (HIBAG; 14,776 cases / 23,047 controls). Stage 1 summary statistics in NIAGADS (accession NG00075), a qualified-access repository.
- Results: Stage 1: 12 GWS loci; λmedian 1.05; LDSC intercept 1.026 (s.e.m. 0.006); observed h2 0.071 (0.011), 0.0637 without APOE. Final sample 35,274 clinical and autopsy-documented AD cases and 59,163 controls. Stages 1+2: 21 GWS associations (incl. TREM2 R47H, ECHDC3, ACE); after Stage 3A, new loci IQCK, ADAMTS1, ACE, ADAM10; Stage 3B adds WWOX (P = 3.7 × 10−8). APOE rs429358 OR 3.32, P = 1.2 × 10−881 (not carried forward). NME8 and MEF2C no longer GWS. Authors note proxy (family-history) cases introduce less sensitivity/specificity than clinically diagnosed AD but can identify relevant loci. Multiple independent signals at TREM2, BIN1, ABCA7, PTK2B/CLU. Pathways: APP/Aβ (P = 4.56 × 10−7), tau binding (3.19 × 10−5), lipid (1.45 × 10−7), immune (6.32 × 10−5), persisting after APOE removal; SNCA (alpha-synuclein) among tau-pathway drivers. HLA-DR15 risk haplotype. rg: maternal family history of AD/dementia 0.81; education negative (college −0.24; years of schooling −0.19 to −0.24); cardiovascular/diabetes measures negative. (Published: final 35,274/59,163 vs preprint 33,692/56,077; Stage 3A/3B sizes differ; APOE OR now reported for the risk coding 3.32 vs preprint 0.30.)
- Discussion: Five new LOAD loci; imputation reveals rare variants (TREM2); APP metabolism implicated in LOAD as well as early-onset AD; tau supports early pathological role; HLA and rg point to shared architecture with immune-mediated and cognitive traits.
- Limitations stated by authors: no dedicated section; rare causal variants may be missed; gene-level causality remains challenging; genetic variance at some loci probably underestimated; WWOX/FST analysed in restricted samples; ADSP sequencing samples overlap ADGC/CHARGE. European (NHW) ancestry only.
- Supplementary material: not available (Supplementary Note with cohort diagnostic criteria referenced only)

## Quality appraisal
Large, peer-reviewed GWAS (Stage 1 n = 63,926; overall n = 94,437) with non-overlapping replication stages and careful QC; clinically/autopsy-diagnosed AD only (no proxy cases), avoiding proxy dilution/survival-related proxy biases but with smaller N than proxy-inclusive GWAS. NHW only. Stage 1 summary statistics are qualified-access (NIAGADS NG00075) — consistent with the manuscript's reason for exclusion (data-use condition itself not described in this text). Superseded in size by Bellenguez 2022. Not MR. Wu 2024 uses this GWAS as the clinical "gold standard" and notes its negative rg with CAD suggesting residual survival bias.

## Relevance to this manuscript
- M1: describes the IGAP clinical AD GWAS (Stage 1 sample, four consortia, NHW ancestry, 1000 Genomes imputation, hg19, NIAGADS qualified-access distribution) — needed only to explain why this dataset was considered and excluded.
- D2: clinical-only AD GWAS vs proxy-inclusive Bellenguez 2022; authors' own statement that proxy cases reduce sensitivity/specificity.
- D1: the AD source of many earlier psychiatric–AD rg/MR studies; its negative rg with education contrasts with proxy GWAX (Wu 2024).
- D5: APP/Aβ, tau, lipid, immune pathways robust to APOE removal; SNCA among tau-pathway genes (only as stated).

## Decision
KEEP — peer-reviewed reference clinically diagnosed (non-proxy) IGAP AD GWAS, cited to justify its exclusion and to frame the clinical vs proxy contrast (M1, D2, D1).
