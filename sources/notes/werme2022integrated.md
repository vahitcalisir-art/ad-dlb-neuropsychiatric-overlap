## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: Global rg averages shared signal across the genome and can miss rg confined to particular regions or of opposing direction at different loci. LAVA tests bivariate local rg, local heritability (univariate) for all traits, and conditional relations (partial correlation, multiple regression). Applied to 20 traits: considerable heterogeneity of local rg often masked by global rg.
- Introduction: Colocalisation methods address single-variant pleiotropy; global rg is a standard GWAS follow-up but an rg does not guarantee causal pleiotropy (LD between distinct causal SNPs). Local rg in opposing directions can produce low/non-significant global rg; strong local rg without a global relationship may go undetected. Existing local methods (rho-HESS, SUPERGNOVA, LOGODetect) are bivariate only. LAVA handles binary and continuous phenotypes with varying sample overlap; the univariate test filters out non-associated loci that give unstable rg.
- Methods: Joint SNP effects estimated from marginal GWAS betas with an LD matrix from a reference panel of matching ancestry (e.g. 1000 Genomes); assumes reference LD equals GWAS-sample LD. LD matrix dimension reduced by PCA (components explaining 99% of variance). Univariate local h2: F-test (continuous) or chi-square test (binary, via reconstructed multiple logistic regression with IRLS). Bivariate local rg from method-of-moments estimate of the local genetic covariance matrix; simulation-based p-values (10,000 default, up to 1,000,000) with partial integration; optional 95% CIs. Sample overlap: user supplies sampling correlation matrix built from cross-trait LDSC intercepts; modelled as residual covariance. Genome partitioned into 2,495 semi-independent ~1 Mb LD blocks (minimum 2,500 SNPs per block). SNP alignment to reference with removal of strand-ambiguous SNPs. Reference: 1000 Genomes phase 3 European subset (N=503). Simulations on 1000 Genomes EUR stacked to N=20,000; LD mismatch deliberately not evaluated. 20 GWAS from GWAS Atlas; INFO<0.9 filtered.
- Results: Unbiased estimates and controlled type I error for binary and continuous phenotypes (Suppl. Figs 2-4); sample overlap bias removed (Suppl. Fig. 5). Univariate filter p < .05/2,495 left 21,374 bivariate tests across 1,919 loci; 546 significant local rg (p < 2.34e-6) across 234 loci, 81 loci with >1 trait pair; 193 with CI for explained variance including 1. Most local rg: BMI-education (39), depression-neuroticism (33). Several trait pairs with near-zero global rg showed significant local rg (Fig. 2), with local rg in opposite directions; alcohol-BMI negative global rg (r = -.3) yet two positive local rg. 229/546 within MHC. Partial correlation (conditioning on asthma) and multiple regression (hypothyroidism) demonstrated in MHC loci (Figs 4-5). Non-MHC hotspot chr11 (NCAM1/DRD2) with depression, neuroticism, alcohol, education, WHR; chr3 locus with insomnia among others.
- Discussion: Genome-wide rg is far from constant; opposing-direction local rg imply more complex aetiology; significant local rg in pairs with non-significant global rg. Pleiotropy vs confounding by LD indistinguishable in LD-dense regions (MHC).
- Limitations stated by authors: can only pinpoint where pleiotropy is likely, confounded by extensive LD, needs experimental validation; analyses one locus at a time, cannot condition on adjacent loci (signal may appear in neighbouring blocks); limited by number of overlapping SNPs across summary statistics; LD mismatch between reference and GWAS sample not evaluated in simulations; univariate filter may exclude loci for low-powered GWAS.
- Supplementary material: not available (only referenced in text)

## Quality appraisal
Methods paper with simulation validation and large application (20 traits). Text is the preprint; the peer-reviewed version is Nat Genet 2022 (cited key year 2022). Content of methods is expected to be largely concordant, but numbers (e.g. 546 local rg, 2,495 loci) and wording may differ in the published version; excerpts below must be re-checked if the published text is cited. No MR. No conflicts of interest declared. Consistent with Shi 2017 (rho-HESS) and Bulik-Sullivan 2015 on the masking argument.

## Relevance to this manuscript
- M2: description of LAVA (univariate local h2 test as filter, bivariate local rg, simulation p-values, sample overlap via LDSC intercepts, reference panel requirement, genome partition into 2,495 blocks).
- I3: genome-wide rg can mask local rg of opposite direction; local rg detected where global rg is null.
- D3: assumption that reference LD matches the GWAS sample; LD mismatch not evaluated (relevant to our use of UK Biobank LD reference and FinnGen sensitivity).
- D6: one-locus-at-a-time limitation, SNP overlap limitation, low power of univariate filter for small GWAS (DLB).

## Decision
KEEP — LAVA is the local rg method used by the study (always KEEP); but note the text file is the bioRxiv preprint, so cite the Nat Genet 2022 version and re-verify excerpts against it.
