## Bibliographic record
Bellenguez C, Küçükali F, Jansen IE, et al.; 2022; New insights into the genetic etiology of Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias; Nature Genetics 54(4):412–436; DOI 10.1038/s41588-022-01024-z; two-stage GWAS meta-analysis (case–control, European ancestry) with pathway, gene-prioritization and GRS analyses; access route PMC-XML (PMC9005347)

## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: Two-stage GWAS totaling 111,326 clinically diagnosed/proxy AD cases and 677,663 controls; 75 risk loci (42 new); amyloid/tau and microglia pathways; 31 tier-1 genes incl. TNF-alpha/LUBAC; new GRS gives 1.6- to 1.9-fold AD risk increase from lowest to highest decile beyond age and APOE ε4.
- Introduction: AD heritability 60–80%; much heritability unexplained; larger GWAS needed. EADB consortium unites European GWAS consortia; new EADB dataset of 20,464 clinical AD cases and 22,244 controls from 15 European countries, meta-analysed with UK Biobank (UKBB) proxy-AD GWAS. Proxy-AD is based on a questionnaire asking whether parents had dementia; less specific than clinical/pathological diagnosis, hence termed proxy AD and related dementia (proxy-ADD).
- Methods: Stage I = EADB, GR@ACE, EADI, GERAD/PERADES, DemGene, Bonn, Rotterdam, CCHS, NxC and UKBB; 39,106 clinical AD cases + 46,828 proxy-ADD cases + 401,577 controls (n = 487,511), 21,101,114 variants; all stage I individuals of European ancestry. UKBB component: 2,447 diagnosed cases, 46,828 proxy cases, 338,440 controls; controls = individuals not reporting dementia or any family history of dementia; analysed with SAIGE logistic mixed model; effect sizes and SEs corrected by a factor of two because proxy cases were analysed (variants MAF > 0.01%, MAC > 3). Other datasets: logistic regression, additive model (SNPTEST/PLINK), adjusted for PCs; imputation with TOPMed (some HRC); UKBB imputed with 1000G/HRC/UK10K. Fixed-effect IVW meta-analysis in METAL; positions in GRCh38 (datasets not on TOPMed lifted from GRCh37); filters on heterogeneity, frequency amplitude, imputation quality. Lambda computed excluding APOE region (44–46 Mb chr19, GRCh38); LDSC intercept computed (LDSC v1.0.1, baselineLD 1000G phase 3, HapMap3 SNPs, APOE excluded; values in Supplementary). Stage II follow-up (APOE region excluded): ADGC, FinnGen and CHARGE, 25,392 AD cases and 276,086 controls. Significance: P ≤ 5e-8 in stage I+II, nominal P ≤ 0.05 and same direction in stage II. Pathway analysis in MAGMA; single-cell enrichment (Allen Brain Atlas); gene prioritization over 7 QTL/annotation domains; GRS (83 non-APOE variants; Cox models in 7 population-based and 8 MCI cohorts).
- Results: 39 signals in 33 known loci + 42 new loci (75 loci). Stage I association magnitudes did not change substantially when restricted to clinically diagnosed AD cases; no signal especially driven by UKBB (Supplementary Table 7). Coloc: TMEM106B and GRN signals likely shared with FTLD-TDP (PP4 99.8% and 80.1%); IDUA PD signal independent. 93 gene sets significant (amyloid, tau, lipids, endocytosis, immunity/microglia); with clinical-only cases 54 gene sets significant, all at least nominal in stage I — authors conclude proxy inclusion does not lose disease-relevant biology. Microglial expression enrichment P = 1.7e-8. 31 tier-1 and 24 tier-2 genes; LUBAC (SHARPIN, RBCK1, OTULIN), TNF-alpha pathway; EGFR at L18, TSPAN14 cryptic exons at L23. GRS HR per allele 1.076 (1.064–1.088) in population cohorts, 1.056 (1.040–1.072) in MCI cohorts; decile HR 1.93 and 1.63. No genome-wide genetic correlation (rg) with other traits is reported in the main text.
- Discussion: Confirms amyloid/tau, innate immunity/microglia; LUBAC and TNF-alpha signalling; shared causal variants with FTD at GRN/TMEM106B may reflect clinical misclassification and proxy-ADD cases in UKBB, or a neurodegenerative continuum. GRS adds modest but consistent prediction. Need for rare-variant, interaction and multi-ancestry GWAS; European-derived GRS less effective in other ancestries.
- Limitations stated by authors: Proxy-ADD less specific than clinical/pathological diagnosis; possible misclassification of clinical AD diagnosis and proxy cases (GRN/TMEM106B overlap with FTD); factor-of-two correction appropriate only for moderate-to-high frequency, small-effect variants; rare variants underpowered; gene–gene/gene–environment interactions unstudied; European ancestry only; FHS in both GWAS stage II and GRS cohorts (sensitivity analysis excluding it).
- Supplementary material: not available (Supplementary Note/Tables, including proxy-ADD definition details and LDSC intercept, not in text file). Summary statistics: GWAS Catalog GCST90027158.

## Quality appraisal
Very large sample (stage I n = 487,511; 85,934 cases incl. 46,828 proxy) — adequate for LDSC/LAVA/MR. Bias risks: ~54% of stage I cases are UKBB proxy-ADD (parental history of dementia), which dilutes specificity, includes non-AD dementias, and introduces a parental/age structure; the factor-of-two correction is an approximation. UKBB controls are not age-screened for future dementia. Sample-overlap risk: UKBB contributes to the AD GWAS, so overlap with UKBB-containing exposure GWAS (e.g., Watanabe 2022 insomnia, UKBB-only) is substantial; FinnGen is in stage II only (relevant if stage I+II statistics were used alongside FinnGen R13). European ancestry only. Peer-reviewed (Nature Genetics). Consistent with earlier Jansen 2019 / Kunkle 2019 / Wightman 2021 loci.

## Relevance to this manuscript
M1: primary AD data source description (sample sizes, cohort composition, European ancestry, GRCh38, UKBB proxy definition, GWAS Catalog accession). D2: explicit author statement that proxy-ADD is less specific and that misclassification/proxy cases may influence findings; also author evidence that clinical-only restriction did not substantially change effects. D6: European ancestry restriction; UKBB inclusion relevant to sample overlap (MRlap) with UKBB-based exposures. D5: microglia/immune, amyloid/tau, TNF/LUBAC pathways for biological interpretation if needed. No rg results reported (does not support I3 directly). M3: not applicable.

## Decision
KEEP — primary AD GWAS data source used in the study (Nature Genetics, peer-reviewed); essential for M1, D2 and D6.
