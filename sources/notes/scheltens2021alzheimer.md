## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract (Brief summary): biologically defined AD three times more prevalent than clinically defined AD; cellular phase; risk of AD 60-80% heritable; causative genes PSEN1, PSEN2, APP, SORL1; APOE4 risk; GWAS ~40 further risk genes; protective APOE2, PLCG2, KLOTHO, APP A673T; plasma biomarkers; lifestyle trials; drug pipeline.
- Introduction: AD main cause of dementia; aim to update 2016 Seminar.
- Methods (Search strategy): Cochrane, PubMed, EMBASE searches Dec 2019 - Feb 2020; narrative selection.
- Results (narrative sections): Diagnostic criteria moved from clinical (NINCDS-ADRDA 1984) to biological ATN framework. Epidemiology: 50 million with dementia (2015); stages of AD at age 70: preclinical 10 y, prodromal 4 y, dementia 6 y; clinical subtype diagnosis often incorrect. Risk factors: main risk factors age and APOE e4; women more likely; modifiable factors about one third of lifetime risk; vascular risk factors may not act via AD pathology. Genetics: twin heritability 60-80%; latest GWAS ~150,000 clinically diagnosed cases/controls and >300,000 by-proxy cases/controls; >40 loci with small effects (OR 1.05-1.2); PRS 75-85% accuracy, mostly due to APOE; pathways: amyloid, immune, cholesterol/lipid, endocytosis, vascular; rare SORL1, ABCA7, TREM2 variants; APOE e2 ~two-fold decreased lifetime risk; PLCG2 P522R reduces AD, DLB and FTD risk. Pathophysiology: microglial genes (APOE, TREM2). Biomarkers: amyloid/tau PET, CSF, plasma p-tau181, NfL. Treatment: pimavanserin for dementia-related psychosis incl. DLB; agitation in up to 70% of AD; suvorexant for insomnia in AD; DMT pipeline (Table 3).
- Discussion (Conclusions): progress in genetics (PRS), biomarkers, trials.
- Limitations stated by authors: none formally stated (narrative seminar); notes reductionist preclinical models and limitations of ATN for clinical practice.
- Supplementary material: not available (Fast facts document referenced only)

## Relevance to this manuscript
I3/D5: APOE e4 as the main genetic risk factor for AD and dominance of APOE in PRS accuracy (supports APOE-excluded sensitivity analysis). D2: explicitly notes that recent AD GWAS combined clinically diagnosed cases with >300,000 by-proxy (parental history) cases/controls, and that clinical subtype diagnosis is often incorrect. I1: agitation up to 70% in AD (minor). D4 (minor): PLCG2 variant shared across AD, DLB, FTD.
