## Bibliographic record
Sankhe K, Vellone D, Guan DX, et al.; 2026; Neuropsychiatric symptoms across the revised Alzheimer's disease clinical staging framework: A review of current evidence and implications; Alzheimer's & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring 18(4):e70510; DOI 10.1002/dad2.70510; narrative review (SANRA, ISTAART NPS-PIA Year-in-Review); access route PMC-XML (PMC13728318)

## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: focused narrative review of 49 (2024) + 28 (2025) MEDLINE publications on AD-related NPS organised by 2024 NIA-AA clinical stages; priorities: diversity, biomarker integration, lived experience.
- Introduction (Background): NPS common, may be harbingers of neurodegeneration; NIA-AA 2024 staging includes NPS; prevalence about 10% in CN, 75-80% in MCI, 76-87% in dementia; MBI defined as persistent later-life NPS (>=50 y) not explained by primary psychiatric disorders.
- Methods: MEDLINE search 1 Jan - 19 Dec 2024, single-reviewer screening, expert survey of ISTAART NPS-PIA (n = 869), 2025 studies via surveillance; not pre-registered; no risk-of-bias assessment.
- Results: 1313 hits, 467 relevant, 77 highlighted. Stage 1: amyloid/tau (amygdala) linked to depressive and anxiety symptoms in cognitively unimpaired. Stage 2: ADNI 16.1% of CN met MBI; NACC CN with MBI had 2.76-fold dementia risk, non-MBI psychiatric symptoms not associated; MBI linked to CSF A+T+ profiles; autopsy: AD pathology but not Lewy body disease associated with MBI. Stage 3: ~60% NPS prevalence in MCI; apathy prevalence 34.1% in 12,646 adults. Stages 4-6: ~40% psychosis in AD dementia; biomarker and imaging correlates; apathy strongest predictor of functional decline in NACC.
- Discussion: stage-dependent role of NPS; strengths (validated tools, diverse cohorts).
- Limitations stated by authors: narrative, non-exhaustive, single reviewer, MEDLINE only, expert-driven selection; many studies lack biomarker confirmation; mostly cross-sectional; specificity of MBI for AD uncertain; needs study in Lewy body disease. Future directions: APOE e4 influences NPS; candidate genes prone to false positives; move to genome-wide/multiomic approaches.
- Supplementary material: not available (Tables S1-S2 referenced only)

## Quality appraisal
Narrative review with single-reviewer selection and no risk-of-bias assessment; restates primary studies. Peer-reviewed. Conflicts: several authors consult for pharmaceutical companies (declared, outside submitted work). No conflict with other sources; its MBI estimate (2.76-fold) is consistent with the Sassi 2026 meta-analysis (pooled HR 2.70).

## Relevance to this manuscript

The MBI-dementia risk claim is covered better by sassi2026mild (meta-analysis), and NPS burden/prognosis in AD and LBD by zhu2026neuropsychiatric (primary cohort).
