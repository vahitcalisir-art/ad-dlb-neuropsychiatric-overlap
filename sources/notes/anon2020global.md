## Section-by-section reading log
- Abstract: GBD 2019 estimates incidence, prevalence, mortality, YLLs, YLDs and DALYs for 369 diseases and injuries in 204 countries, 1990–2019; age-standardised DALY rates improved; depressive disorders sixth leading cause of DALYs at 25–49 years; ischaemic heart disease and stroke top causes at ≥50 years.
- Introduction: Rationale for regular, comprehensive descriptive epidemiology; GBD 2019 supersedes GBD 2017.
- Methods: 86 249 data sources; CODEm, ST-GPR, DisMod-MR 2.1; bias correction of alternative case definitions by network meta-regression; standard locations for covariate estimation; new method for assigning dementia as underlying cause of death (excess mortality share in end-stage disease); SDI; UIs from 1000 draws.
- Results: Global DALYs ~2540 million in 2019; age-group-specific leading causes (Figure 2); Alzheimer's disease and other dementias in top ten only at ≥75 years; depressive disorders among top drivers of increasing burden; national epidemiological transition (Figure 3) and SDI trends (Figure 4). Cause-specific prevalence for individual diseases is in printed two-page summaries and online tools, NOT in this text file.
- Discussion: Opioid overdose, hepatitis C elimination in Egypt, congenital syphilis, headache disorders, dementia prevention evidence limited, growing YLD share.
- Limitations stated by authors: Primary data availability; reliance on out-of-sample modelling; difficulty representing full uncertainty; dementia death reassignment needs replication; severity distributions for mental/neurological disorders not geographically varied; inconsistency between mortality and prevalence data in DisMod.
- Supplementary material: not available (appendices referenced only)

## Quality appraisal
Authoritative, peer-reviewed (Lancet), huge data base; but the main text gives no prevalence figures for AD, DLB, MDD, PTSD, insomnia or schizophrenia. Model-based. Not MR.
